Words to know

Sap
The watery liquid that moves through a maple tree. It is mostly water with a little sugar.
Tapping
Drilling a small hole in the trunk and fitting a spout so the sap can drip out into a bucket or a tube.
Freeze-thaw cycle
Nights below freezing followed by days above freezing. The changes in pressure push sap through the tree.
Sugar bush
A stand of forest where maple trees are tapped to make syrup.
Syrup
What you get when sap is boiled until most of the water has gone. It takes roughly 40 litres of sap to make 1 litre of syrup.
